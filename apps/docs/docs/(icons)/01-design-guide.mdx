These rules describe how the Vadivam icons are drawn. The numbers come from measuring every icon in `icons/` with `bun scripts/icon-geometry.mjs`. Where a rule is not met by every icon, the share that meets it is given. Rules marked **Checked** fail `bun run icons:check`; the rest are reviewed by eye.

## Principles

- **One grid.** Every icon is drawn on the same 24×24 canvas with the same 2px stroke, so icons line up and match in weight when they sit next to each other.
- **Crisp at 24px.** Straight edges land on whole pixels at 1×, so the stroke renders as solid pixels rather than a blur.
- **Soft, generous shapes.** Base shapes fill the canvas and use large corner radii with 60% corner smoothing, the same curve as iOS. Vadivam's square is 20×20 with a smoothed 6px radius, where Lucide's is 18×18 with a circular 2px.
- **Lucide's vocabulary, redrawn.** Every icon is a new drawing of a Lucide icon under the same name, so a Lucide name works in Vadivam unchanged.
- **Clarity over rules.** When a rule hurts how readable an icon is, readability wins. The exceptions below are listed by name.

- **Checked.** The canvas is `24×24` with `viewBox="0 0 24 24"`.
- **Checked.** Keep 1px of padding. With round caps and joins, a 2px stroke reaches 1px past its centerline in every direction, so ink stays inside 1–23 and centerlines stay inside 2–22. All 1,903 icons pass.
- Use the live area. 81.6% of icons reach the padding line on at least one side, and the median icon's ink is 22px across its larger dimension. Only 12.6% stay inside 2–22. Line arrows are the exception; see [Arrows](#arrows).
- Center the drawing. In 98.6% of icons, the center of the ink's bounding box is within 0.5px of (12, 12). Asymmetric icons may shift for optical balance, as arrows do.

## Stroke

- **Checked.** Draw with a single `2px` stroke, `stroke="currentColor"`, `stroke-linecap="round"`, and `stroke-linejoin="round"`, all set once on the root `<svg>`.
- Keep the stroke centered on the path, which is how SVG renders it. Draw open centerlines and never outline a stroke into a filled shape.
- **Checked.** A path with more than 8 closed subpaths is rejected, since that usually means outlined stroke geometry. The most in any icon today is 3.
- **Checked.** No fills: the root sets `fill="none"`, and source artwork with any other `fill` is rejected before it is optimized.
- Do not mix stroke widths or emulate a thicker line with parallel strokes. Consumers change the weight with `strokeWidth`; see [Usage](/docs/usage).

A 2px stroke centered on a whole-number coordinate covers exactly two pixel rows at 24px, so it renders solid. Centered on a half pixel, the same stroke covers one full row and two half rows, and renders blurred. So, for crisp edges:

- Put horizontal and vertical lines on whole-pixel coordinates. 93.6% of the set's 6,933 horizontal and vertical straight segments do (94.4% of those at least 2px long). In 87.7% of icons, every one does.
- **Checked.** A horizontal or vertical line at least 1px long that sits less than 0.1px off a whole pixel is an export slip, not a choice. `bun run icons:optimize` snaps it onto the whole pixel, along with every point that shares its coordinate, so strokes meeting it move with it. Lines 0.1px or more off, such as those on half pixels, are left as drawn.
- Start rectangles on whole pixels: 96.4% of rectangle corners do.
- Center circles on whole pixels where you can (75.6%), or on half pixels (13.6%) when an odd-sized detail needs it.
- Let curves and diagonals follow the shape. Only 44.4% of all vertices are on whole pixels, because arc and curve endpoints land wherever the shape puts them, and smoothed corners start part-way along each edge. Of 3,120 diagonal segments, 39% run at 45°.
- Path data keeps at most 3 decimal places. `bun run icons:optimize` rounds it.

An arrow is an open shape, so drawn to the padding it looks larger than a circle or square of the same width. Line arrows stay inside the live area:

- A single arrow has a 16px shaft and a head 14px across and about 6.5px deep. The head keeps Vadivam's swept shape: a short straight tip at 35° to the shaft that curves outward into each barb.
- Paired arrows, such as `arrow-left-right`, and the arrow in sort icons use the narrow head, 8px across and 6px deep. Sort icons keep a 20px shaft so it matches the letters beside it.
- Diagonal arrows fit a 12×12 box, so their length matches a straight arrow's.
- A bar sits 4px from the tip or tail, as in `arrow-right-to-line` and `arrow-right-from-line`.
- Center an arrow optically. The head carries most of the ink, so a geometrically centered arrow looks shifted toward where it points. Move it toward its tail until the center of its ink box and its ink centroid fall on either side of (12, 12), about equally far from it: 1px for single arrows and `to-line` arrows, 0.5px each way for diagonals. Arrows with a bar at the tail are already balanced.

### Corner smoothing

- **Checked.** Smooth every rounded corner at 60%, the same curve as iOS and Figma's iOS preset. A smoothed corner leaves the straight edge on a cubic curve, turns through a shorter arc of the same radius, and eases back onto the next edge, so the bend has no sudden start. `bun run icons:optimize` applies it to every circular arc that meets two straight lines tangentially, using the construction in `scripts/corner-smoothing.mjs`, and `bun run icons:check` rejects any such corner left as a plain arc. 2,880 of the set's 2,881 such corners are smoothed; the last is below.
- Leave room for it. A 60% corner reaches 1.6 times its radius along each edge, so it fits when the radius is at most the edge ÷ 3.2 (6.25 on a 20px side, 2.5 on an 8px side). Where it doesn't fit on an edge of 8px or more, optimize tightens the radius to the largest 0.25px step that does: a 20×18 frame gets r 5.5, and an 8×8 tile gets r 2.5. 2,189 corners (76.0%) are at the full 60%.
- On edges under 8px, and at corners sharper than 90°, the radius is kept and the corner gets as much smoothing as the edge allows, as Figma does. Tightening a sharp corner would push its tip outward. These 691 corners have a median smoothing of 33%. A corner with room for less than 5% smoothing keeps its plain arc, since that little is invisible: the r 7 corner in `webcam-off` has only 0.05px of straight edge on each side.
- Some arcs are not corners and stay circular: bends that turn less than 30°, arcs with no straight line on one side (pill ends, curved stroke ends), and corners where another stroke attaches at the end of the arc, such as the pins on some `calendar-*` icons and folded file corners.

Lucide uses circular 2px corners on shapes 8px and up and 1px below. Vadivam's larger, smoothed radii are what give it its softer look, so don't copy Lucide's corners.
