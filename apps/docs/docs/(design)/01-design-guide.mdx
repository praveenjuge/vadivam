These rules describe how the Vadivam icons are drawn. The numbers come from measuring every icon in `icons/` with `bun scripts/icon-geometry.mjs`. Where a rule is not met by every icon, the share that meets it is given. Rules marked **Checked** fail `bun run icons:check`; the rest are reviewed by eye.

## Principles

- **One grid.** Every icon is drawn on the same 24×24 canvas with the same 2px stroke, so icons line up and match in weight when they sit next to each other.
- **Crisp at 24px.** Straight edges land on whole pixels at 1×, so the stroke renders as solid pixels rather than a blur.
- **Soft, generous shapes.** Base shapes fill the canvas and use large corner radii. Vadivam's square is 20×20 with a 6px radius, where Lucide's is 18×18 with 2px.
- **Lucide's vocabulary, redrawn.** Every icon is a new drawing of a Lucide icon under the same name, so a Lucide name works in Vadivam unchanged.
- **Clarity over rules.** When a rule hurts how readable an icon is, readability wins. The exceptions below are listed by name.

- **Checked.** The canvas is `24×24` with `viewBox="0 0 24 24"`.
- **Checked.** Ink stays on the canvas. With round caps and joins, a 2px stroke reaches 1px past its centerline in every direction, so no coordinate may sit closer than 1px to an edge. All 1,899 icons pass.
- Keep 1px of padding: ink stays inside 1–23, so centerlines stay inside 2–22. 1,896 of 1,899 icons (99.8%) do. The exceptions reach 0.16–0.25px into the padding: `columns-3-cog`, `heading-6`, and `message-circle-question-mark`.
- Use the live area. 82.5% of icons reach the padding line on at least one side, and the median icon's ink is 22px across its larger dimension. Only 11.7% stay inside 2–22.
- Center the drawing. In 98.9% of icons, the center of the ink's bounding box is within 0.5px of (12, 12). Asymmetric icons may shift for optical balance.

## Stroke

- **Checked.** Draw with a single `2px` stroke, `stroke="currentColor"`, `stroke-linecap="round"`, and `stroke-linejoin="round"`, all set once on the root `<svg>`.
- Keep the stroke centered on the path, which is how SVG renders it. Draw open centerlines and never outline a stroke into a filled shape.
- **Checked.** A path with more than 8 closed subpaths is rejected, since that usually means outlined stroke geometry. The most in any icon today is 6.
- **Checked.** No fills: the root sets `fill="none"`, and source artwork with any other `fill` is rejected before it is optimized.
- Do not mix stroke widths or emulate a thicker line with parallel strokes. Consumers change the weight with `strokeWidth`; see [Usage](/docs/usage).

A 2px stroke centered on a whole-number coordinate covers exactly two pixel rows at 24px, so it renders solid. Centered on a half pixel, the same stroke covers one full row and two half rows, and renders blurred. So, for crisp edges:

- Put horizontal and vertical lines on whole-pixel coordinates. 93.6% of the set's 8,816 horizontal and vertical straight segments do (94.5% of those at least 2px long). In 86.7% of icons, every one does.
- Start rectangles on whole pixels: 95.7% of rectangle corners do.
- Center circles on whole pixels where you can (73.0%), or on half pixels (13.6%) when an odd-sized detail needs it.
- Let curves and diagonals follow the shape. Only 58.3% of all vertices are on whole pixels, because arc and curve endpoints land wherever the shape puts them. Of 3,258 diagonal segments, 40% run at 45°.
- Path data keeps at most 3 decimal places. `bun run icons:optimize` rounds it.

- Round every rectangle. None of the 299 axis-aligned rectangles in the set has a sharp corner.
- Make the radius about 30% of the rectangle's shorter side, on a 0.5px step where you can. The median ratio is 0.30; 80% of rectangles fall between 0.25 and 0.50.
- For sides of 8px or more, the common radii are 6 (83 rectangles), 4 (36), 3 (32), and 2.5 (24).
- For sides under 8px, the common radii are 2 (40), 2.5 (20), and 1.5 (15). A 4px-wide bar becomes a pill with r 2.
- Use arcs rather than approximating curves where a corner is circular. The set has 6,742 circular arc commands with a median radius of 3.5.

Lucide uses 2px corners on shapes 8px and up and 1px below. Vadivam's larger radii are what give it its rounder look, so don't copy Lucide's corners.

Separate parts of an icon either join or leave a readable gap:

- Leave a 2px gap between separate elements by default. In the set, 73.4% of separate parts that come within 6px of each other keep at least 2px; 2px is the most common gap (2,099 pairs).
- Never go below 1.5px. 1.5px is the second most common gap (1,535 pairs), used where 2px doesn't fit, such as the dot under a bar. In 85.5% of icons, every gap is at least 1.5px.
- Don't leave a near miss. If two strokes come closer than 1.5px, join them. 63 icons, including `image`, `earth`, and `train-front-tunnel`, have a stroke ending 0.3–2px short of another, so the strokes merge into a blob without meeting.

These figures measure the gap between separate subpaths: their centerline distance minus the 2px stroke. Gaps within a single subpath, such as between the turns of a spiral, are not measured.

## Naming

- **Checked.** Every file name is a current canonical Lucide name from the pinned release, recorded as `lucideVersion` in `metadata/lucide.json` (currently 1.60.0). Deprecated aliases are rejected: use `circle-alert`, not `alert-circle`.
- **Checked.** Names are lowercase kebab-case: letters and digits separated by single hyphens.
- **Checked.** Every icon has tags and at least one known category in `metadata/lucide.json`, taken from Lucide's metadata for the same name.
- Component names are the PascalCase form of the file name, generated by the build: `circle-alert.svg` becomes `CircleAlert`. Never name a component by hand.

## What's not allowed

`bun run icons:check` and `bun run icons:optimize` reject an SVG that breaks any of these rules:

- **Root.** The root `<svg>` has exactly `xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg"`, `width="24"`, `height="24"`, `viewBox="0 0 24 24"`, `fill="none"`, `stroke="currentColor"`, `stroke-width="2"`, `stroke-linecap="round"`, and `stroke-linejoin="round"`. Any other root attribute, such as `class`, `id`, or `style`, is rejected.
- **Elements.** Only `<path>`, `<circle>`, `<line>`, `<polyline>`, `<polygon>`, `<rect>`, and `<ellipse>` are allowed. That rules out `<a>`, `<clipPath>`, `<defs>`, `<filter>`, `<foreignObject>`, `<g>`, `<image>`, `<linearGradient>`, `<mask>`, `<pattern>`, `<radialGradient>`, `<script>`, `<style>`, `<symbol>`, `<text>`, `<use>`, and any other element. Every icon in the set uses only `<path>`.
- **Attributes.** Each element carries geometry only: `d` on a path; `cx`, `cy`, and `r` on a circle; `x1`, `y1`, `x2`, and `y2` on a line; `points` on a polyline or polygon; `x`, `y`, `width`, `height`, `rx`, and `ry` on a rect; and `cx`, `cy`, `rx`, and `ry` on an ellipse. No `fill`, `stroke`, `transform`, `opacity`, `style`, `class`, `id`, or event handlers.
- **Geometry.** Every element needs geometry. An icon needs at least one drawable element. Path data must parse. A single path may hold at most 8 closed subpaths. The stroke must stay on the canvas.
- **Fills.** Source artwork with a `fill` other than `none` is rejected before optimization, so outlined Figma exports never reach the set.
- **Files.** File names must be canonical Lucide names in lowercase kebab-case, with an entry in `metadata/lucide.json`.

Treat every SVG as untrusted input: these checks run before anything is published or deployed.

## Accessibility

An icon is either decorative or meaningful. It is decorative when it repeats nearby text, such as an icon next to a button label. It is meaningful when it carries information on its own, such as an icon-only button or a status indicator.

- Hide decorative icons from assistive technology. The web framework components add `aria-hidden="true"` when no title or accessible attribute is given.
- Give a meaningful icon a name with `title`, `aria-label`, or, in React Native, `accessibilityLabel`. Name an icon-only button on the button itself.
- Don't rely on an icon's shape or color alone to carry state.

See [Usage: accessibility](/docs/usage#accessibility) for examples in each framework.
